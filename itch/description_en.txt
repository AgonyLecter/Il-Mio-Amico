IL MIO AMICO (My Friend)
A cute game. I promise.

One morning you wake up with an itchy wrist.
There's a tiny black dot on it. Smaller than a mole.

The next day it's a lump.
The day after, it opens its eye.

And then it says hi.

It's small, it's round, it has purple hair and it's sooo hungry. It just wants to be your friend. Feed it. Pet it. Talk to it. Go to work so you can buy it food. Keep it happy.

What could go wrong?

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

★ A short pixel-art horror story in 5 chapters (about 30-45 minutes per run)
★ Your choices are remembered. Even between runs.
★ 6 endings to discover (plus some that are not exactly endings)
★ Tiny minigames: feed it, pet it, work, resist...
★ It knows what time it is. It knows when you leave. It knows you.
★ Online: see how many hosts are awake right now, what percentage of players chose like you, and leave a message on the mirror for the next host
★ Fully playable in English and Italian

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

HOW TO PLAY
• Phone: play in landscape. Tap to continue, tap the options to choose.
• PC: click / SPACE / keys 1-9 to choose. M = audio, F = fullscreen.
• Use headphones. Play at night. Alone, if you can.

Your progress is saved automatically in your browser. On the title screen you get a host code to continue on another device.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CONTENT WARNING
Body horror, blood, self-harm imagery (in a fictional context), psychological horror and fourth-wall breaking. Recommended for ages 16+.
The game may use vibration and read your battery level and clock, only to make the experience more personal. Nothing leaves your device except your chosen name, your choices (anonymous stats) and the optional message you leave on the mirror.
